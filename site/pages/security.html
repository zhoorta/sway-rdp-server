threat model–□✕

The login is the account's password inside TLS, with no second factor: keep the port off the open internet. The daemon has no privileges, a small root helper starts the sessions, and each account is isolated from the others. This is not an audited product: four internal review passes are written down, with what is still open.

  • docs/THREAT-MODEL.md: who is assumed hostile, and the open items
  • docs/SECURITY-REVIEW.md: the internal reviews
  • docs/ACCESS.md: who can reach the port, certificates, the VPN
